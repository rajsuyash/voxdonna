How it works

Live in three steps. One of them is a click.

No new call platform, no recording bot joining as a guest, no change to how your team already sells.

Step 01

Upload the playbook

Pricing sheets, battle cards, objection handling, the deck your best closer actually uses. Rocket reads it and answers only from it.

Step 02

Add the extension

One browser extension per rep. It spots the call starting; nothing else changes about the meeting your prospect sees.

Step 03

Take the call

The objection lands, the answer appears beside it, sourced. Your rep reads a line they'd have taken a week to learn.

What it does

Your best closer, on everyone's call.

The gap between your top rep and your median rep is recall under pressure. That's a solvable problem.

⚡

Answers in 1.4 seconds

Fast enough to use mid-sentence. A suggestion that lands after the topic moved on is a transcript, not a coach.

📎

Grounded, not guessed

Every line traces back to a real page in a document you uploaded. No invented pricing, no discount nobody approved.

🛡️

Objection reframes

Price, timing, incumbent, "send me something in writing". It recognises the move and hands over the counter.

📝

Recap before the next call

Managers see the film
