The problem

The founder is the operations team.

You take the call, write the proposal, chase the follow-up, and research the next prospect at eleven at night. An assistant is a hire you can't justify yet, and a general chatbot doesn't know a thing about your business.

🧠

A chatbot forgets you

Every session starts from zero. You re-explain your pricing, your positioning and your customers before you get one useful paragraph.

📚

Your knowledge is scattered

The real answer is in a deck from March, a contract in Drive, and a note on your phone. None of them talk to each other.

🔓

Shared AI tools leak

Most multi-tenant AI is one prompt away from mixing your data with somebody else's. That is a risk you can't take with client work.

Isolation

Every tenant is alone in the building.

Multi-tenant AI fails quietly. Ours was built assuming that, then attacked until it held: separate storage per tenant, row-level rules on the database, and a model backend with no operator context, no shell, and no file access. Each control was demonstrated failing against a deliberately vulnerable build before it was trusted — a probe that only ever passes is decoration.

1
Isolation is tested, not asserted

The tenant-isolation suite runs on every deploy and blocks the release if a single probe comes back clean when it should not.

2
Missing credentials fail loudly

A skill without its integration returns a clear 503 and says which one it needs. Nothing else in the product is affected, and nothing degrades silently.

3
You can be your own tenant

Single-tenant deployment on your own infrastructure, if your clients' data requires it.

# pre-deploy gate typecheck: PASS row_level_isolation: PASS tenant A query for tenant B rows → 0 model_backend.context: PASS no operator context reachable model_backend.shell: DENIED model_backend.files: DENIED storage_scope: PASS production_build: PASS # every check is able to fail
