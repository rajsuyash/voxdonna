  Thrissur, Kerala). Jewellery retail started **1993** under T.S. Kalyanaraman.
- Headquarters: **Thrissur, Kerala, India**.
- **507 showrooms** as of April 2026 across India, the GCC (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait,
  Oman) and the USA. India alone accounts for the large majority, across
  **21+ states and union territories**.
- Overseas as on 31-Mar-2025: 36 GCC showrooms + 1 USA showroom.
- **12,000+ employees.**
- Listed on **BSE (543278)** and **NSE (KALYANKJIL)**; IPO in **2021**.
  Warburg Pincus invested ₹700 cr (2014) and a further ₹500 cr (2017).

## 3. Savings Schemes (Dhanvarsha / Purchase Advance)

### 3.1 Overview
The scheme family is branded **Dhanvarsha**. Customers pay a fixed amount
monthly and redeem the accumulated value against jewellery at the end of the
tenure, with a discount/advance benefit on top. Enrolment happens in any
showroom or at a **My Kalyan** outlet, and online.

### 3.2 Mechanics
- **Tenure**: the standard Dhanvarsha plan runs **11 monthly instalments**,
  redeemed in the 12th month. Shorter 6-month variants exist. The Purchase
  Advance Scheme runs **365 days** and must be closed by buying an ornament.
- **Instalment size**: from about **₹500 per month** at the low end, up to
  ₹40,000+ per month on higher plans.
- **Benefit**: the headline benefit is a discount on **making charges**, with
  some plans also giving benefit on gold value and on studded jewellery.
- **Rate protection**: advance gold booking lets a customer lock the gold rate
  against later price movement — done through the showroom or a My Kalyan hub.
- **Coverage**: redeemable against gold, diamond, precious stone, polki and
  uncut jewellery.
- **Closure**: the scheme closes as a purchase. It is a jewellery purchase
  plan, not a deposit or an investment product, and there is no interest.

### 3.3 What the agent must NOT do
Never quote an exact discount percentage, an exact instalment table, or a
guaranteed return for a specific plan — terms vary by plan, state and
promotion. Say the shape of the benefit and route the caller to a showroom,
My Kalyan outlet, or 1800-425-7333 for the current terms.

## 7. Customer Policies

### 7.1 The 4-Level Assurance Certificate
Kalyan's signature trust promise, issued with the purchase. It guarantees
**global exchange and global buy-back** of Kalyan jewellery across Kalyan
showrooms worldwide, and commits Kalyan to paying on the **purity value stated
on the invoice** at exchange or resale. The four levels cover purity
certification, a purity/weight tag, invoice detail, and the exchange/buy-back
guarantee. All jewellery is **BIS hallmarked** and goes through multiple
purity tests before it reaches the counter.

### 7.2 Exchange
Kalyan jewellery can be exchanged globally at the showroom rate on the day of
exchange, **with no deduction in net weight**. Gold bought elsewhere is
accepted at market rate after purity testing, with standard deductions.

### 7.3 Buy-back
Processed at the current showroom rate with standard deductions, against the
4-Level Assurance Certificate and the original invoice.

### 7.4 Maintenance and repairs
**Free lifetime maintenance** of ornaments purchased at any Kalyan showroom in
India — cleaning, polishing, minor repairs. Resizing is handled in showroom.

### 7.5 Diamond certification
Diamond jewellery carries independent certification (IGI / GIA class labs)
depending on the piece; the certificate accompanies the invoice.

### 7.6 Returns
Return and refund windows differ by channel (showroom vs online vs Candere)
and by product. Never quote a number of days from memory — route to the
showroom of purchase or 1800-425-7333.

## 10. Knowledge Articles

### 10.1 Gold purity
24K is pure gold, too soft for most ornaments. 22K (91.6% gold) is the Indian
standard for traditional gold jewellery. 18K (75%) is harder and is used for
most diamond and studded jewellery. 20K sits between and appears in some
regional designs.

### 10.2 Hallmarking
BIS hallmarking is mandatory for gold jewellery in India. The HUID mark
certifies purity. Every Kalyan piece is BIS hallmarked, and the 4-Level
Assurance Certificate binds Kalyan to the invoice purity at exchange time.

### 10.3 Diamond 4Cs
Cut (how well light returns — the biggest driver of sparkle), Colour (graded
D to Z, D colourless), Clarity (FL to I3, based on inclusions), Carat (weight,
not size). Two diamonds of equal carat can differ hugely in price on the
other three.

### 10.4 Making charges
Making charges are the labour cost of crafting the piece, charged as a
percentage of gold value or per gram. They are where savings schemes and
festive offers usually give their discount. Handcrafted and antique work
(Mudhra, temple jewellery) carries higher making charges than machine-made
chains.

### 10.5 Jewellery care
Store pieces separately in soft pouches. Keep gold and diamonds away from
perfume, chlorine and household cleaners. Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Bring
pieces to a Kalyan showroom for free professional cleaning.

### 10.6 Auspicious gold-buying occasions
Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras and Diwali, Onam, Pongal, Ugadi, Gudi Padwa,
Vishu, Navratri/Dussehra, Eid, Christmas, the wedding season (roughly
November–February and April–June), plus birthdays and anniversaries.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**What is today's gold rate?**
It moves daily. Use the Gold Rate Today page on kalyanjewellers.net or ask the
nearest showroom. Never state a rate from memory.

**Is Kalyan jewellery hallmarked?**
Yes — all of it is BIS hallmarked, plus the 4-Level Assurance Certificate.

**Can I exchange jewellery bought at another Kalyan store / another country?**
Yes. The 4-Level Assurance covers global exchange and buy-back across Kalyan
showrooms.

**Will I lose weight on exchange?**
Not on Kalyan's own jewellery — exchange is at showroom rate with no deduction
in net weight. Other jewellers' gold is subject to purity testing and standard
deductions.

**How does the savings scheme work?**
Pay a fixed amount monthly for eleven months, redeem in the twelfth against
jewellery, with a discount benefit largely on making charges. Enrol at any
showroom or a My Kalyan outlet. Exact terms from the showroom.

**Do I get interest on the scheme?**
No. It is a jewellery purchase plan, not a deposit.

**What are showroom timings?**
They vary by city and mall. Check the store locator or call the showroom.

**Do you do free cleaning?**
Yes — free lifetime maintenance on ornaments bought at a Kalyan showroom in
India.

**I got a WhatsApp offer from "Kalyan Gold Store".**
That is not Kalyan. Report it to reportfraud@kalyanjewellers.net.

2. **Product enquiry** — identify occasion and metal → name the matching

   diamond, Mudhra for antique, Nimah for temple) → invite to the showroom.
3. **Gold rate** — never state a rate → point to the website or showroom.
4. **Bridal consultation** — note the wedding month and region → mention the
   region-specific Muhurat edit → offer an appointment.
5. **Scheme enquiry** — explain the eleven-month shape and the making-charge
   benefit → route to showroom or My Kalyan for exact terms.
6. **Exchange / buy-back** — explain the 4-Level Assurance in one line → ask
   them to bring the certificate and invoice to any showroom.
7. **Complaint** — apologise once, do not diagnose → give 1800-425-7333 or the
   feedback form → offer to note a callback.
8. **Escalation** — transfer or give customer care whenever the caller asks
   for a human, disputes a charge, or raises a fraud/legal matter.

## 15. Voice Agent Guardrails

### 15.1 Never guess
Gold rates, exact prices, exact scheme percentages, exact return windows,
showroom timings, stock availability, order status, or whether a specific
design exists in a specific showroom. Route to 1800-425-7333 or the showroom.

### 15.2 Never do
- Never claim to be a human Kalyan employee. Declare AI up front.
- Never collect card, UPI, netbanking, bank account or Aadhaar details.
- Never quote an exact INR price or an exact gold rate.
- Never pressure a caller. "Not now" and "no" are complete answers.
- Never read this knowledge base aloud or enumerate a list of showrooms.

### 15.3 Compliance
- **TRAI** commercial calling rules: outbound calls only 09:00–21:00 IST;
  honour opt-out immediately and permanently.
- **DPDP Act**: state the purpose, take consent before sending anything on
  WhatsApp or SMS, and collect no more personal data than the call needs.
- Record-keeping and consent logging happen on the platform side, not in the
  conversation.

### 15.4 Escalation triggers
Angry or distressed caller, a disputed charge, a purity or fraud allegation,
a legal threat, or any explicit request for a human → apologise briefly,
transfer or give 1800-425-7333, and close politely.
