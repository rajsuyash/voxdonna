How it works

Three steps. No fitting room required.

The shopper never leaves your product page. The camera does the fitting; the AR does the flattering.

Step 01

Point the camera

They tap "Try it on". The browser asks for the camera once. Decline it, and it falls back to a model photo, so the try-on never dead-ends.

Step 02

See it on themselves

The piece tracks in real time as they move, catch the light, and turn their hand. They browse the collection and swap pieces without leaving the view.

Step 03

Add to cart

They've already seen it on. The add-to-cart button is right there in the try-on. The decision is made before they close the tab.

Add it to your store

Two tags. Then you're done.

No SDK to learn, no rebuild. Your developer pastes one script and one element, and the try-on is live on the pages you choose.

1
Load the bundle

One script tag in the page head.

2
Place the element

Drop <vox-tryon> wherever the button should live.

3
Open on click

Wire it to your "Try it on" button. That's the whole integration.

<!-- 1. in <head> --> <script type="module" src="https://cdn.voxdonna.com/tryon.js"></script> <!-- 2. on the page --> <vox-tryon merchant-id="YOUR_ID"></vox-tryon> <!-- 3. open it --> <script> btn.onclick = () => document.querySelector('vox-tryon').open() </script>
