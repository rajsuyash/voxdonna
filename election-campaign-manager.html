Five core use cases + one bonus

What Voxdonna does for campaigns.

From first-touch persuasion to Election Day GOTV — every conversation captured, every outcome synced to your booth dashboards.

USE CASE 1 OF 5

Voter Outreach & Persuasion Calls

Reach every voter in target booths — conversationally, at scale.
STEP 01
Segmentation & Targeting
Import voter list booth-wise. Mark supporter/neutral/unknown from VRM or panna canvassing. Prioritize "unknown" and "soft supporter" voters in key booths.
STEP 02
Compliant Conversational Script
Identify + consent opener. Short issue-based pitch (roads, water, welfare, local development) pre-certified by MCMC. 2-3 simple questions on support level, top issue, follow-up preference.
STEP 03
Data Capture & Sync
Store call outcome with voter ID + booth ID. Nightly sync to booth management/VRM so panna pramukhs know exactly whom to contact in person.
STEP 04
Human Follow-Up Loops
Auto-assign "persuadable" or "requested human" voters to ground agents. Provide full conversation summaries for seamless handoff.
Replaces first-touch manual calling with scalable AI conversations — while strengthening the panna pramukh model, not replacing it.
USE CASE 2 OF 5

Booth Management & GOTV

Turnout maximization — booth by booth.
PRE-POLL
Booth Consolidation
Voxdonna calls all identified supporters to reconfirm support, verify voter slip details, and check constraints (mobility, work hours). Outcomes written back to booth-level dashboards.
POLLING DAY
GOTV Reminders
For booths with low midday turnout, trigger reminder calls to known supporters about polling closing time and available assistance. System auto-throttles to respect MCC silence periods.
REAL-TIME
Booth-Wise Turnout Intelligence
Call-through data per booth (answer rates, "already voted" confirmations) becomes a soft real-time indicator of lagging turnout — helping campaigns redeploy volunteers dynamically.
Value to parties: improved booth-wise visibility, tighter coordination with human booth teams, and more systematic GOTV — within the law.
USE CASE 3 OF 5

Voter Helpline & Grievance Redressal

Always-on, AI-powered campaign helpline.

What Voxdonna Handles

  • FAQs on polling date, voting hours, voter ID requirements, polling station location
  • Candidate manifesto info and rally schedules (approved content only)
  • Logging complaints: name missing from roll, booth access issues — tagged by booth and issue type

Routing & Escalation

  • Urgent complaints (malpractice, law & order) immediately escalated to human operators or legal teams
  • Aggregated helpline data shows concentration of grievances for operational response
  • Single campaign helpline number per assembly, promoted on leaflets, social, and outreach
An always-on helpline increases perceived responsiveness and transparency — increasingly expected by voters during elections.
USE CASE 4 OF 5

Volunteer Coordination & Booth-Team Management

Voxdonna as your internal operations agent.
ONBOARDING
Volunteer Confirmation
Outbound calls to confirm availability, preferred role (booth agent, data entry, mobilizer), training session slots, and language comfort.
TRAINING
Training Reinforcement
Short automated training micro-lessons via outbound calls — how to fill forms, 100-meter zone rules, handling voter questions — with simple voice/keypad quizzes captured.
RALLIES & MEETINGS
Logistics Reminders
Automated reminders to volunteers about timings, locations, and responsibilities for rallies, padyatras, public meetings, and Election Day shifts.
Reduces load on constituency managers — lets booth-level leadership focus on high-value political work instead of basic coordination calls.
USE CASE 5 OF 5

Surveys, Perception Tracking & Issue Mapping

Data-driven intelligence for strategy and messaging.
PERCEPTION
Quick Perception Polls
Short, statistically designed surveys via outbound calls to randomly selected voters per booth — satisfaction with governance, priority issues, candidate awareness.
STRATEGY
Issue Tagging for Content
Open-ended responses classified by topic (jobs, inflation, roads, water, law & order) — enabling data-driven decisions on themes for speeches, ads, and manifestos.
A/B TEST
Message Testing
A/B versions of approved campaign messages tested on small random samples. AI measures recall and favorability from follow-up responses, feeding back to creative teams.
Keep this in the "insights and optimization" zone — no micro-targeting of protected groups. Only issue-based aggregate analytics for refining legal messaging.
BONUS USE CASE

Leader Voice Calls — Motivation & Feedback at Scale

Deploy the voice of political leaders to energize workers and hear directly from voters.

For Internal Support Staff (Motivation)

  • Synthetic leader voice messages delivered to booth workers, panna pramukhs, and volunteers before key campaign milestones
  • Personalized by name and role: "Ramesh ji, your booth in Sector 12 is critical — the party is counting on you"
  • Pre-election pep calls, post-canvassing appreciation messages, Election Day morning motivation blasts
  • Drives morale and accountability across thousands of distributed ground workers without requiring the leader's actual time

For Voters (Feedback Collection)

  • Leader-voice outbound calls to soft supporters and undecided voters asking for honest feedback on local issues and candidate performance
  • Higher pickup and engagement rates when voters believe they are hearing from a known leader
  • Structured feedback captured: satisfaction score, top concern, likelihood to vote — synced to VRM
  • Responses aggregated into constituency-level insight reports for the campaign war room
Compliance Gate: Synthetic leader voices must be (a) explicitly approved in writing by the party and candidate, (b) labeled as AI-generated audio per ECI/MeitY advisories, (c) pre-certified via MCMC before any bulk deployment, and (d) never used to impersonate a leader in real-time or deceptive contexts. Voxdonna enforces this gate — no launch without documented clearance.

Regulatory & design

Built for ECI compliance — by design.

Compliance is not a constraint. It's your competitive moat against ad-hoc IVR vendors and grey-market AI tooling.

Regulatory Constraints

  • Bulk voice calls under ECI's pre-certification regime — treated as electronic political ads
  • MCMC approval required at district/state level for all voice messages and digital media
  • Model Code of Conduct: no religious/caste appeals, no defamatory content, silence period restrictions
  • ECI & MeitY advisories on AI: responsible use, labeling of synthetic audio/video, quick takedown of manipulated content

Voxdonna's Built-In Safeguards

  • MCMC-first workflow: each campaign object requires valid approval reference before launch
  • Language packs by state: Hindi + state language bundles with code-mixing support
  • Transparent AI identity: agent always declares itself as automated. No leader impersonation without explicit written clearance
  • Hard content guardrails: MCC-prohibited content blocked at prompt and retrieval layer
  • Call logging and pacing caps to prevent abuse
These choices de-risk your campaign tech stack and make Voxdonna a serious, compliance-aware partner for national and state-level parties.
