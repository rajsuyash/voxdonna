VoxDonna · FieldVoice

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

FieldVoice is a field‑meeting recording and intelligence application operated by DONNA.AI LABS PRIVATE LIMITED ("VoxDonna", "we", "us") and provided to organisations for use by their field teams.

The organisation that provides FieldVoice to you — typically your employer — is the data controller for your information, and VoxDonna acts as a data processor on its instructions. This policy explains what the FieldVoice app collects and how it is handled, and applies to the FieldVoice application — which may be listed on the App Store under an organisation‑specific name.

1. Information we collect

  • Account & identity. Your name and work email, obtained when you sign in with your organisation's Microsoft (Entra ID) account. FieldVoice does not create, see, or store your password.
  • Meeting recordings. Audio you record during customer meetings — captured only after an explicit on‑screen consent step at the start of each recording.
  • Derived content. Transcripts, AI‑generated summaries, and action items produced from those recordings.
  • Meeting details. The customer, date and time, and participants you associate with a meeting.

2. How we use your information

Solely to provide the service: to transcribe recordings (including multilingual audio), identify speakers, generate summaries and action items, and make the results available to you and your authorised managers within your organisation. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

3. Where your data is stored — data residency

All meeting data is processed and stored exclusively in India (Amazon Web Services, ap‑south‑1 region). Specifically:

  • Audio stored on your device is encrypted with AES‑256. Once it uploads, the local copy is deleted.
  • Audio in the cloud is private and accessible only through short‑lived, signed links.
  • Data is isolated per organisation and per role at the database level, so users only see what their role permits within their organisation.

4. AI processing

Transcription and summarisation are performed using the Sarvam, Bhashini, or Amazon Transcribe APIs operating within India. We do not use any other AI providers, and we do not send your data outside these processors.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not use any third‑party analytics, advertising, or crash‑reporting services in the app. The app communicates only with your organisation's FieldVoice service (in India) and with Microsoft for sign‑in.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Sharing

Your information is visible only to you and to your authorised managers within your organisation, and is processed by the AI transcription providers named in Section 4, acting strictly as our sub‑processors within India. Cloud infrastructure (hosting, storage, and transactional email) is provided by Amazon Web Services in the India region under our control. We share your data with no other parties, and never with data brokers.

7. Retention

Local device audio is deleted after it successfully syncs. Server‑side data is retained for as long as your account is active and in accordance with your organisation's data‑retention policy. On request from your organisation or you, data can be deleted.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at admin@voxdonna.com or your organisation's administrator. Because your organisation is the data controller, some requests may be fulfilled through it.

9. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, on‑device AES‑256 encryption of recordings, per‑organisation data isolation, and role‑based access controls.

10. Children

FieldVoice is a workplace tool intended for use by employees of the organisations that deploy it. It is not directed to, or intended for use by, children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by notice to the organisations that use FieldVoice.
