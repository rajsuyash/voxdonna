Before you choose

A few things,
answered.

Have a specific workflow in mind? Talk it through with us.

What happens after I choose a plan?

You go to checkout for the plan you selected. After purchase, connect your channels, add your business information, and test Donna’s replies and booking flow before going live. Setup time depends on your channels and calendar.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?

You can link your existing number through the WhatsApp connection flow. If you need the official WhatsApp Business API, talk to us about your setup before purchasing. Connection methods and requirements differ by channel.

What does a credit cover?

Credits power Donna’s AI replies. The pricing estimates use two conversations per credit. Actual usage varies by conversation, and additional credits are charged separately. Review your balance and recharge settings in the dashboard.

Can my team take over a conversation?

Yes. Set the situations that need a person, and take over with the conversation history available. Review Donna’s instructions and test the handoff before going live.

Does she book directly into my calendar?

Appointment booking is included on Plus, Growth, and Max. With a supported calendar connected, Donna checks availability and books appointments in the conversation. Test your calendar connection and booking rules during setup. Contact us before buying if you need a specific calendar integration.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel anytime. Every plan comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. Email hello@voxdonna.com within 14 days of purchase to request a refund.

Can Donna reply in my customers’ language?

Donna supports English, Hindi, and regional languages. Test the languages your customers use with your own business information before launch.
