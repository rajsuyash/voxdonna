FAQ

The questions you're about to ask.

How fast can I go live?

Minutes. You pick a plan, your account is ready the moment you pay, you scan a QR to connect WhatsApp, and Donna builds her playbook from your website. No call with us, no waiting.

How does Donna connect to my WhatsApp?

You scan a QR code with the WhatsApp number you already use — the same way WhatsApp Web works. Nothing leaves your phone, and you can disconnect any time. Instagram, Messenger, web chat, and SMS connect the same way, all into one inbox.

Will my number get banned?

Used sensibly on an established number, you're fine. New numbers need a short warm-up before high volume — we walk you through it in setup. If you want Meta's official WhatsApp Business API instead (higher limits, no warm-up), that's supported too; it just needs Meta verification first.

What are credits, and what happens when they run out?

Credits power Donna's AI replies. Each plan includes a monthly amount — a typical conversation is a few credits. If you run low, auto-recharge tops you up so she never stops mid-chat, or you top up manually. You're never billed by surprise.

Does Donna book straight into my calendar?

She checks real-time availability, confirms the slot in the chat, and writes it to your calendar in seconds. The customer never leaves the conversation. When a chat gets complicated, she hands off to a human with the full thread attached.

Can I cancel? What if it's not for me?

Cancel any time, no contract. And every plan is backed by a 14-day money-back guarantee — if Donna isn't for you, email us within 14 days for a full refund.

What languages does she speak?

English, Hindi, and major regional languages. She switches to match the customer mid-conversation.
