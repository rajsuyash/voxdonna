FAQ

The questions you're about to ask.

Which channels does Donna cover?

WhatsApp and Instagram DMs lead the way. Messenger, web chat, and SMS run on the same agent, so every conversation lands in one inbox.

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. Donna runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta. Your number stays compliant, verified, and safe from bans. No grey-market automation.

Does Donna book straight into my calendar?

She checks real-time availability, confirms the slot in the chat, and writes it to your calendar in seconds. The customer never leaves the conversation.

What happens when a conversation gets complicated?

She hands off to a human with the full thread attached. Your team picks up exactly where she left off. Nobody starts from zero.

How fast can Donna go live?

14 days. We connect your channels, link your calendar and data, design the flow, run test conversations, then switch it on.

What languages does she speak?

English, Hindi, and major regional languages. She switches to match the customer mid-conversation.
