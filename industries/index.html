Before you start

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Which workflow should we start with?

Choose a frequent enquiry or follow-up with a clear next step and reliable source information. We help you define the scope, human handoff and success measure before building. Start with one workflow, then expand based on what the pilot shows.

Do we need to replace our CRM, ERP or phone number?

We scope the agent around your existing tools and the access they provide. A pilot can begin with approved documents and a structured handoff. Live lookups, bookings and system updates require the relevant connections and agreed permissions.

What will a pilot cost, and how long does it take?

The proposal sets out the build cost, ongoing usage costs, channels, integrations and launch plan before work starts. Scope and timing depend on the workflow, enquiry volume, approved content and system access.

What if our industry is not listed?

Tell us which conversation or process repeats most. We can assess the workflow, the information it needs and the point where a person should take over. The demo library also includes examples beyond the industries shown here.
