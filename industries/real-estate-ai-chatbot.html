The questions
worth asking.

What does the real estate AI chatbot actually ask a buyer?

It opens on WhatsApp and asks for configuration, budget, location and preferred timeline, the same brief you can see captured in our property demos. A voice follow-up call qualifies further and confirms a site visit time where a calendar is connected.

Which CRMs does the chatbot connect to?

We scope the integration against your CRM, API access and required fields. That can include Sell.Do, LeadSquared, Salesforce or Zoho, with the buyer brief, conversation summary, lead status and assigned salesperson written across. A structured export can support a pilot where a live connection is not ready.

Which languages can the chatbot and voice follow-up use?

Try the English and Hindi voice demo and the French property walkthrough on this page. We confirm your required languages, tune pronunciation and test real buyer questions with your team before launch.

Does a live salesperson ever take over from the chatbot?

Yes. Pricing negotiations, discounts, unit holds and any question outside the approved answers are routed to your sales team with the buyer's context attached. The chatbot is built to hand off, not to close the deal alone.

Do we need the official WhatsApp Business API?

You can link your existing WhatsApp number through the connection flow. If you need the official WhatsApp Business API, talk to us about your setup before purchasing, since connection methods and requirements differ by channel.
