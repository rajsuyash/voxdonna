## How to choose by business type

**Indian multi-store showroom.** Your leak is usually the phone and WhatsApp during busy floor hours. You need Indian languages, gold rate and scheme answers from your own data, and booked visits. Look at VoxDonna first, and at Converiqo if you want a broader operations platform.

**Luxury boutique.** Volume is lower, and each client matters. A guided WhatsApp agent (Engati) or a clienteling and communication platform (CrownSync, H&CO) may fit better than a high-volume phone agent. Keep a person on every high-value conversation.

**D2C jewelry ecommerce.** Your catalogue lives in Shopify or a similar store. Alhena, Rep AI and Shoplabs are built for that setup. Pick Shoplabs if the pain is inbound calls, and Alhena or Rep AI if it is on-site conversion.

**Manufacturer or wholesaler.** Your callers are retail partners, not shoppers: exhibition invitations, payment follow-ups, order status and collection launches. Most tools above are shopper-facing. VoxDonna's [manufacturer page](/jewellery-manufacturers.html) covers the partner desk.

Many jewellers end up with two pieces: an agent for conversations, and a separate marketing partner. That is fine if their data and handoffs are clear. The [VoxDonna jewellers hub](/jewellers.html) lays out the conversation side.

## What to ask any vendor

- Which channels does it handle live: phone, WhatsApp, web chat, email? Ask to hear or try each one.
- Which languages are tested for my customers, including mid-sentence switching?
- Where does the gold rate come from? What happens when the rate is missing or stale?
- Which systems does it read from: ERP, POS, CRM, stock extract, calendar? Is the connection live or a scheduled export?
- What does it do when it does not know? Does it hand off with context, or guess?
- Who owns the build, the data and the conversation logs if I leave?
- How is pricing structured: platform fee, per conversation, per minute, build fee? What happens at Dhanteras volume?
- Are outbound calls limited to opted-in customers, inside permitted calling hours, with AI disclosure and permanent opt-out?
- Can I see a result from a business like mine, and is the number self-reported?

### Can an AI agent quote today's gold rate?

Yes, if it is connected to an approved rate feed from your business. If the rate is missing or out of date, the agent should hand the question to your team rather than guess. Ask every vendor how it handles that case.

### Do AI voice agents replace showroom staff?

No. They take the routine calls and messages, such as rates, timings, stock and scheme questions, and book visits, so your advisors stay with the customer at the counter. Bridal and high-value conversations should still reach a person.
