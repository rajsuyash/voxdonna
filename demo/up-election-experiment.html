    Outbound

    A batch script reads the voter list and dials at a configurable rate. Each call streams Hindi audio in the campaign voice profile while the conversational AI engine handles the dialogue end-to-end. Sub-second latency, full duplex.

    Inbound feedback

    Before hang-up, the agent persists 9 structured fields per voter — name, constituency, sentiment, top issues, vote intent, follow-up consent. These records aggregate into the live dashboard, sliceable by district, age band, sentiment or issue.

    Compliance

    कानूनी रूप से सुरक्षित

    TRAI TCCCPR, DPDP Act 2023, and IT Rules 2021 — every requirement is either built into the system or operator-gated by design. The synthetic-leader-voice gate is the deepest control: no production rollout without written candidate/party approval, MCMC pre-certification, and ECI/MeitY-compliant AI-audio labelling.

    ✓Built-in

    TRAI TCCCPR

    DND-registered numbers scrubbed before campaign load. Opt-outs logged and honoured permanently across future campaigns.

    ✓Built-in

    DPDP Act 2023

    Consent recorded in-flow for follow-up contact. Voter PII stored separately from feedback analytics. 72-hour breach reporting wired.

    ✓Built-in

    Calling hours

    Outbound dialler only operates 09:00–21:00 IST. Demo route hard-blocks calls outside this window.

    ⚠Operator-gated

    MCC silence period

    Operator disables the dialler during the 48-hour pre-poll silence period. Manual gate — visible in operator console with a hard timer.

    ✓Built-in

    Audit trail

    Every call carries a metadata source tag (leadership_demo vs production). Full transcripts retained for inspection by election authorities.

    ⚠Operator-gated

    Synthetic leader voice

    No production rollout without (a) written candidate/party approval, (b) MCMC pre-certification at district/state level, (c) ECI/MeitY-compliant AI labelling on every turn. Voxdonna enforces this gate at the platform level — no launch without documented clearance.
