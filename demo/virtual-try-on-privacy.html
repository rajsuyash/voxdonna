Virtual try-on privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This notice covers the VoxDonna virtual try-on demo, operated by Donna AI Labs Private Limited. It explains the demo’s camera and image processing. The terms for our voice and messaging products do not describe this demo.

What you share

After you select the consent checkbox, press Start try-on and grant browser permission, your camera video is streamed to our AI processing provider. The selected clothing or jewellery image and your outfit description are also sent to generate the preview. The demo does not request or use your microphone.

Only share images you have permission to use. Keep other people and confidential information out of the camera view.

Who processes the preview

VoxDonna provides the demo. Decart AI supplies the video-generation service. Your browser connects to that service to send the video and receive the generated result; processing is not confined to your device. Provider processing may occur outside your country. We do not promise India-only storage for this demo.

The provider’s published API terms permit use of inputs and outputs for service improvement and AI model training. We have not verified a separate no-training arrangement for this demo. Do not submit material you are unwilling to have processed on that basis.

Recording, retention and technical data

The VoxDonna demo code does not record or save your camera session, uploaded item image or generated video on our server. This does not mean the processing provider keeps no data. Its published privacy policy allows retention and exceptions for legal obligations and claims; we have not confirmed a fixed deletion period for this demo’s provider-side data.

Our session endpoint stores a hashed IP address and request timestamps to limit abuse. Entries older than one hour are removed when a subsequent accepted request rewrites the rate-limit file, so expiry is not a guaranteed deletion time. Hosting and service providers may also process connection and operational logs. Loading the page requests supporting files, including fonts and the video SDK, from external services.

Your controls and requests

You can browse the demo without starting the camera. Press Stop camera to end streaming. The demo also stops when you leave or hide the tab and limits each session to two minutes. Ending a session stops further streaming; it does not itself delete data already processed by the provider. You can revoke camera permission in your browser.

For questions, withdrawal of consent, access, correction or deletion requests, contact admin@voxdonna.com. Include the approximate session time so we can investigate and coordinate with the provider where needed. Requests are subject to applicable rights and lawful retention requirements. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

This is VoxDonna’s notice for this integration, not a renamed copy of the provider’s policy. Ask us for the current provider information or a copy of the relevant terms without leaving VoxDonna. We will update this page when the demo’s data handling changes.
