About Voxdonna

Why we built an AI voice agent that sounds like a real person, not a robot.

The problem we saw

Most businesses lose calls before they can answer them.

Inbound call abandonment sits at 20–40% across the industries we work with — voicemail dead-ends, hold-time hang-ups, after-hours silence. Every missed call is a lead, a booking, a renewal someone else gets to win.

SMBs can't justify a 24/7 receptionist. Enterprise contact centers can't scale headcount with demand spikes. And the existing tools — IVR menus, chatbots, scripted dialers — fall apart the moment a caller goes off-script. "Press 1 for sales" is not a customer experience. It's a tax on the customer.

What Voxdonna does

A 24/7 voice agent that picks up on the first ring.

Donna handles inbound and outbound calls across 12+ verticals — hospitality, healthcare, manufacturing, supply chain, B2B sales, multilingual support, care homes, aftermarket, industrial IoT. She sounds human, holds context across the conversation, reads from your real knowledge base, and books, qualifies, or escalates without dropping the call.

Live in 14 days. Pay per minute used. No seat licenses, no six-month integration, no professional services contract.

Founder

Founded by Suyash Raj.

Background in growth and product at consumer and B2B SaaS companies. Building Voxdonna because the gap between "AI voice agent demos" and "AI voice agents that actually convert" was wider than anyone in the category was willing to admit.

Why voice over chat? Because for the workflows we care about — booking a table, dispatching a tech, qualifying a lead, taking a warranty claim — voice is still the channel where deals close. Chat is where customers wait. Voice is where they decide.

What makes us different

Four things we don't fake.

  • Real demos, not mockups 12 live agents on /demos. Talk to them in your browser. No video reels, no scripted call recordings.
  • Multilingual native English, French, and Italian on the same call. Donna detects and switches mid-conversation.
  • Knowledge base per agent Real product info, real FAQs, real escalation rules — wired to your data, not a generic LLM hallucinating prices.
  • 14-day deploy Not a six-month integration project. We build, you test, you go live within two weeks of kickoff.
