About Voxdonna

Every business is a stack of conversations it cannot keep up with.

We build the AI agents that keep up. On the phone, inside WhatsApp, on the sales floor, and in the field.

Why we exist

The money is in the conversations nobody had time for.

The call that rings out at 9pm. The WhatsApp read on Monday, one day too late. The bridal enquiry that walked into the store that answered first. The sales meeting that lived in one rep's memory until it did not. None of it shows up in a dashboard. All of it costs you.

The old fixes made it worse. Phone menus, scripted chatbots, and dialers that fall apart the moment a customer goes off-script. "Press 1 for sales" is not service. It is a tax on the customer.

What we are

A company that builds custom AI agents, to spec.

Voxdonna is a full-stack AI consulting company. We find where AI moves a number, then design, build, and run the agent that does it. On voice, on WhatsApp, in sales meetings, and on campaigns. Live in weeks, in 22 Indian languages, wired to your real data instead of a generic model inventing your prices.

31 agents shipped across 12 industries. Not slideware. Live agents you can talk to in your browser right now.

Why Donna

We named her after the person who already knows.

Every great operation has a Donna. She remembers the client's daughter's name and the deal that closed two years ago. She reads the room, handles the thing before you asked, and never once sounds like a machine. That is the bar. Every agent we ship is built to clear it.

Founder

Founded by Suyash Raj.

Built Voxdonna because the gap between an AI demo and an AI that actually converts was wider than the category would admit. The demos looked great. The deployments never shipped. So we built the unglamorous part: agents that go live, stay compliant, and move a number a CEO can point to.

Why across channels, and not one neat product? Because your customer does not care which channel they are on. They call, they message, they walk in. The agent should be there for all three, and remember them across all three.

Co-founder & CTO

Vipul Shaily, co-founder and CTO.

Twenty years building software, now aimed at one thing: agents that hold up in production, not just in a demo. Sub-second latency, integrations that do not break, and the unglamorous reliability work that decides whether an AI project ships or quietly dies.
