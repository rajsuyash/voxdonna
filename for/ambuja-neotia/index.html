02 — Architecture

How Voxdonna plugs into your sales lounge.

Four layers. Each one designed so the next morning, the Upohar sales advisor opens her laptop and sees a clean queue — not a transcript dump.

LAYER 01 · TELEPHONY FRONT

Indian numbers, anti-spam, DLT registered.

One Voxdonna-licensed inbound number per project (or a single masterline routed by project mention). Outbound on TRAI DLT-registered headers. DPDP-compliant written-consent capture inside the call flow. DND scrubbing on outbound campaigns.

  • Inbound: 24/7 pickup, sub-300ms answer
  • Outbound: DLT template per campaign
  • Language detection at first turn
  • Caller-ID + history fetch (when known)
LAYER 02 · CONVERSATIONAL AI

Donna voice. Scope-locked. KB-grounded.

ElevenLabs multilingual voice (the same orb you're testing above), routed through GPT-class reasoning with a hard scope guard.   • Sub-second response latency

Right sales lounge, structured payload.

One email, one dashboard, zero scrolling.

Hot leads (budget + timeline ≤ 3m) marked at the top. Out-of-scope routes (hospitality, healthcare, TNU) summarised separately for vertical owners.

USE CASE 1 OF 5 · HERO

24/7 Multilingual Property Enquiry Agent

The line that's open when the sales lounge isn't.
STEP 01 · GREET
Identify caller intent in seven seconds.
Donna picks up, switches to the caller's language (Bengali / Hindi / English), asks which project they're calling about — or offers a shortlist if they're undecided. Captures name and callback number.

Configuration · budget · timeline.

Hold a site-visit slot at the project sales lounge.
Mon–Sat, 11:00 / 14:00 / 16:30. Donna holds the slot pending advisor confirmation the next morning. SMS or WhatsApp confirmation goes out before the call ends. Virtual 3D walkthrough link offered for distant callers.
STEP 04 · SYNC
Write structured lead to the sales lounge.
Payload posted to CRM (or shared sheet) within 30 seconds of call end. Morning brief at 08:00 IST surfaces overnight leads sorted by hotness. Out-of-scope queries (hospitality, healthcare, TNU) routed separately.
Business win. Recovers the 109-hour after-hours window without adding a single seat. At ~4,400 after-hours enquiry calls/month and a 25% site-visit conversion rate, the math is in the ROI section below.

06 — Compliance & data residency

Built for DPDP — and the eleven other regulators your legal team will ask about.

DPDP Act 2023 compliance commences this year. Penalties run up to ₹250 crore per breach. Real estate, hospitality and healthcare are all in scope. We scraped a dozen voice-AI vendor sites pitching India — not one of them mentioned DPDP by name. We treat it as the moat, not the footnote.

Regulatory ground

  • DPDP Act 2023. Written consent capture at first call, data residency for personal data, defined retention, breach reporting under 72 hours.
  • TRAI DLT. All outbound campaigns ride registered headers and pre-approved templates. DND scrub before every campaign.
  • Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations. No outbound between 21:00–10:00. Inbound 24/7 is unrestricted.
  • Sector-specific. For hospitality: no spam under Ministry of Tourism guidelines. For healthcare: clinical-advice firewall, never a diagnosis or medication suggestion. For real estate: pricing only from the verified sales advisor — never the agent.

Voxdonna safeguards

  • India region voice infrastructure. Calls processed in Mumbai region. Audio and transcript storage in-country.
  • Hard scope guard. The agent refuses clinical advice, legal advice, investment-return predictions, and any pricing commitment. It captures and routes.
  • Consent capture in flow. First-turn consent line on outbound. SMS/email opt-out honoured on next contact attempt.
  • Auditable transcripts. Every call logged, redacted where required, exportable for DPDP audits.
  • "Demo agent" disclosure. If a caller asks directly, the agent acknowledges it is an AI demo. No deceptive impersonation.
The compliance posture is not a bolt-on. It's the reason Ambuja Neotia would short-list Voxdonna over the dozen generic voice-AI vendors that pitch Indian conglomerates without ever mentioning DPDP, TRAI, or sector-specific guardrails. We treat it as the competitive moat.
