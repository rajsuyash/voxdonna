05 — Compliance & clinical safety

Built for DPDP — and the NMC Telemedicine Practice Guidelines.

DPDP Act 2023 compliance commenced enforcement this year. Penalties up to ₹250 crore per breach. Indian clinics buying Clinora are gating vendors on this specifically. The voice channel has the highest risk surface — and the largest possible upside if handled right.

Regulatory ground

  • DPDP Act 2023. Written consent capture at first call, data residency for patient personal data, defined retention, 72-hour breach reporting. Doctor as data fiduciary; clinic obligations cascade to the voice provider.
  • NMC Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020. No clinical advice, diagnosis, or prescription by non-physicians. The agent captures and routes — never advises. Hard guard at the prompt layer.
  • TRAI DLT. All outbound campaigns (follow-up, vaccination reminders, pre-consult triage) ride registered headers and pre-approved templates. DND scrub before each campaign.
  • Clinical Establishments Act. Patient communication logs auditable per state-level inspection norms.

Voxdonna safeguards

  • India region voice infrastructure. Calls processed in Mumbai region. Audio + transcript storage in-country.
  • Hard clinical-safety guard. Agent refuses diagnosis, medication advice, dosage advice — every time. Captures and routes to the doctor.
  • Emergency-triage trigger. Chest pain, breathlessness, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding → immediate 108 routing + RED flag.
  • Consent capture in flow. First-turn consent line on outbound. SMS/email opt-out honoured.
  • Auditable transcripts. Every call logged, redacted where required, exportable for DPDP audits and NMC reviews.
  • "AI demo" disclosure. Agent acknowledges if asked. No deceptive impersonation of a clinician.
Your homepage already names HIPAA + GDPR — strong US/EU posture. DPDP is the India-specific gap. We treat it as the moat, not the footnote. None of the voice-AI vendors pitching Indian healthtech today mentions DPDP by name. HealthBridges + Voxdonna together can.
