The problem

Generic outbound marketing has stopped working.

Plain SMS gets buried. Robocalls get hung up on. Generic IVR menus feel like a tax on the customer. Real celebrity endorsements are too expensive for most companies, and even for big brands they are slow to produce and hard to personalize at scale.

The result: low pickup rates, weak recall, no emotional connection — and a marketing channel that's quietly underperforming for almost every brand.

The solution

AI Bollywood-style voices at scale.

Voxdonna gives brands the power to run outbound calling campaigns using Bollywood-inspired AI celebrity voices. Every call is personalized with your customer data — name, city, last purchase, preferred language — wrapped inside a warm, emotionally familiar voice persona.

This makes premium voice-led marketing accessible to both SMEs and large enterprises: the kind of emotional engagement that used to cost crores, now available as a software platform.

How it works

From persona to launch in five steps.

STEP 01

STEP 02

STEP 04

Track outcomes
Monitor pickup, engagement, conversions, and interest signals. Iterate every campaign.
