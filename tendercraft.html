The problem

Most bids die on paperwork. Nobody read the technical part.

A compliant response takes three or four people the better part of a week. Then it is thrown out for a missing undertaking, an expired certificate, or an annexure in last month's format.

📕

Three hundred pages, one deadline

Eligibility clauses, evaluation matrices and prescribed annexures scattered across an NIT, its schedules, and however many corrigenda have landed since.

🚫

Rejected for insufficiency

Document insufficiency kills the bid before merit is assessed. One unanswered criterion is enough, and you find out months later.

💸

No pre-bid visibility

Tender fee, EMD handling, and staff weeks spent on a tender the eligibility clause ruled you out of on page eighteen.

How it works

Read the tender. Check the shelf. Write only what you can prove.

Three moves, in order. Nothing in step three can reference something that did not survive step two.

Step 01

Lock the tender model

Every criterion is extracted with its clause reference, page and paragraph, then labelled mandatory, desirable, or self-attestation. Low-confidence extractions wait for you. The model locks before anything is drafted, and corrigenda re-open it.

Step 02

Verdict per criterion

Turnover, net worth, completion dates and certificate validity are compared arithmetically against your stored profile. Pass, Fail, or Needs-review, each with the clause that produced it and the gap in rupees or months.

Step 03

Draft with the receipts

Criterion-mapped narrative, each sentence carrying a citation into your own content library. Compliance matrix, annexure index and cover pages are rendered, not written. Missing information becomes a flag, never a plausible sentence.

Where the line sits

AI reads and writes. Arithmetic decides.

A model is good at finding the clause and bad at being trusted with the verdict. So it never holds one. This split is enforced in the code, not in a policy document.

Traceability

The export gate is the product. Everything else is convenience.

Speed is what every competitor sells. The claim here is narrower and harder: nothing leaves this system unsourced. A generated sentence without a resolvable citation blocks the export. A mandatory criterion with no answer blocks the export. A financial figure a model wrote instead of pulled blocks the export. You can override it — with your name attached.

1
Cite or flag

Every claim resolves to a document in your library, or it becomes an explicit gap. Invention is not one of the outputs.

2
Figures are pulled, not written

Turnover, contract values and dates transclude from structured records. A model never types a number into your bid.

3
No portal credentials, ever

There is no autonomous submission in any version. The platform assists the export; a person files it.
