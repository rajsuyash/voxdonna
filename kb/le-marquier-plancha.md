## Why a Plancha vs a Grill

| Feature | Plancha | Grill |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Flat polished steel | Open grates |
| Heat | Even, edge to edge | Hot/cold spots |
| Best for | Delicate fish, seafood, veg, eggs | Thick steaks, burgers |
| Flare-ups | None | Frequent |
| Cleaning | Scrape warm, no soap | Brush grates |
| Preheat | **5–8 min** | 10–20 min |

A plancha is the chef's secret for a perfect Saint-Jacques sear or whole sea bass — without losing a morsel through the grates.

## Care & Maintenance

1. **Before first use** — coat the chrome surface with neutral oil and heat 15 minutes to season.
2. **After cooking** — while warm, scrape residue, splash a little water to deglaze, wipe clean. **Never use soap** — it strips seasoning.
3. **Light oil coat** after each clean keeps the surface dark and non-stick.
4. **Cover when not in use** — extends life dramatically.
5. **Replace the burner kit** (€79) if you notice uneven heat — a 15-minute DIY job.

## FAQs

**1. Gas or electric — which?**
Gas (60, 75, 90, Mini) for outdoor cooking and true open-air flavor. Electric (Pro Indoor) only if you cook indoors — and only that model is rated for indoor use.

**2. Can I leave my plancha outside year-round?**
Not unprotected. Always use a cover, and in extreme cold store indoors. Moisture is the enemy of steel.

**3. Butane or propane — what works?**
Both. Most gas models ship butane-ready and convert to propane or natural gas with a **€29 kit**. Propane works straight from the tank.

**4. How long does it take to preheat?**
About **5 to 8 minutes**. The 90 Reception runs closer to 8; the Compact is ready in 5.

**5. Can I cook a steak on a plancha?**
Absolutely. Edge-to-edge contact gives ribeye or entrecôte a uniform crust — many customers say they will never go back to a grate.

**6. Is the surface non-stick?**
Not Teflon — **seasoned**. With proper oiling, food releases cleanly. Trust the seasoning.

**7. Do you ship to the UK?**
Yes, **DDP** — customs and VAT handled by the carrier, no surprise fees on delivery.

**8. Compact vs Familial?**
Surface (60×35 vs 75×40 cm), wattage (3.5 vs 5.4 kW), party size (1–2 vs 4–6). The Familial adds a third zone and side shelves.

**9. Can I use any of these indoors?**
