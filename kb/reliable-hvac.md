## Response Time Tiers

### Tier 1 — Emergency (24/7)
On-site within 2 to 4 hours. Triggered by:
- No heat in winter (October through April)
- Suspected gas leak
- Active water leak or pipe burst
- Sewage backup
- Carbon monoxide alarm

### Tier 2 — Urgent
Same business day or next morning. Triggered by:
- No AC during summer heat
- Water heater failure
- Drain blockage with no working alternate fixture
- Partial heating loss

### Tier 3 — Standard
Scheduled within 5 business days. Covers:
- Routine maintenance and tune-ups
- New installations and replacements
- Quotes and estimates
- Non-urgent repairs

## Safety Guidance (Critical)

Donna must give this guidance verbatim when relevant:

- **Smell of gas**: Leave the building immediately, do not flip switches, call 911 from outside, then call us at 617 555 0143.
- **Carbon monoxide alarm**: Leave the building immediately, call 911, then call us. Do not re-enter until cleared.
- **Active flooding**: Shut off the main water valve (usually in the basement near the meter), then call us.
- **No heat in winter**: Keep faucets dripping slightly to prevent pipe freeze, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, set thermostat above 55 degrees if any heat is available.
- **Electrical burning smell from HVAC**: Turn the unit off at the breaker and call us.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How fast for a no-heat call in winter?**
A: Tier 1 emergency. Technician on-site within 2 to 4 hours, 24/7.

**Q: Do you offer free estimates?**
A: Yes for new installs and full system replacements. Repair diagnosis is $89, waived if you approve the repair.

**Q: Do you handle oil-to-gas conversions?**
A: Yes. Full conversion including oil tank removal, gas line install, and boiler or furnace commissioning. We pull all permits.

**Q: What size AC do I need?**
A: BTU sizing is calculated on-site using a Manual J load calculation that factors square footage, insulation, windows, ceiling height, and sun exposure.

**Q: Do you offer financing?**
A: Yes. 12-month no-interest on jobs over $1,500 via Wells Fargo Home Projects. 60-month financing for full system replacements.

**Q: Do you service rental properties?**
A: Yes, with landlord authorization on file. Tenants can call us, but work is invoiced to the landlord.

**Q: Are you licensed and insured?**
A: Yes. License MA HVAC-12345, $2M liability, full workers' comp.

**Q: Do you offer annual tune-ups?**
A: Yes. Bronze at $189/year includes one visit. Silver $349 includes two. Gold $599 includes two plus 15% off repairs.

**Q: Boiler failed at 11 PM. What now?**
A: Tier 1 emergency. 24/7 dispatch with 2 to 4 hour response. After-hours fee is $189, applied to repair if you proceed.

**Q: Do you handle permits?**
A: Yes. We pull permits for boilers, water heaters, gas line work, and AC installs.

**Q: What refrigerants do you work with?**

**Q: Do you replace tankless water heaters?**
A: Yes. Navien, Rheem, and Bosch units. Typically a one-day job.

**Q: My drain is backing up. Is that an emergency?**
