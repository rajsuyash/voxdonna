## How It Works (5 Steps)

### Step 1 — Choose the Voice Persona
Brand selects a voice persona from the library based on the audience and campaign mood. Sample calls played back before final selection.

### Step 2 — Connect Customer Data
Voxdonna integrates with the brand's CRM, spreadsheet, or customer database. Each outbound call pulls live data — first name, city, last order, preferred language — and the AI voice speaks it naturally inside the persona.

### Step 3 — Design the Call Flow
Teams design campaigns for offers, reminders, partner activation, loyalty outreach, or event-based follow-ups. Voxdonna provides templates plus a script builder.

### Step 4 — Launch at Scale
Once approved, Voxdonna places thousands or millions of calls while maintaining a human-like, emotionally engaging voice experience. Telephony, retries, throttling, and opt-out handling all managed by the platform.

### Step 5 — Track Outcomes
Brands monitor pickup rates, engagement (call duration, response to questions), conversions (offer redemption codes), and interest signals (callback requests). Data exports to the brand's CRM for retargeting.

## Compliance and Trust

Celebrity-style AI voices must be handled responsibly. Voxdonna ships these as default:

- **Licensed or approved voice personas only**: no unlicensed clones of real public figures.
- **Transparent AI usage disclosure** where regulation requires it (DPDP Act in India, GDPR for EU customers).
- **Consent-aware outbound calling**: every campaign runs only against verified opt-in lists. Aligned with TRAI commercial calling rules and DND.
- **Opt-out and suppression controls** baked into every campaign.
- **Brand safety guardrails**: no impersonation of real celebrities or political figures.
- **DPDP-compliant data handling**: customer data processed per India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
