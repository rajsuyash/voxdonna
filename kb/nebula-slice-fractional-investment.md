## Why Fractional Ownership (the pitch)

- Traditional vacation homes need crores upfront, sit idle most of the year, and burden the owner with upkeep, staff, and taxes.
- Fixed deposits and similar instruments offer modest returns and no lifestyle value.
- Fractional ownership gives a low entry point, professional management, steady passive income (~9.5%), capital appreciation, and luxury stays — without the headache of full ownership.
- It democratises access to premium 5-star real estate that was previously limited to high-net-worth buyers.

## How It Works (4 steps)

1. Invest — buy a slice of a premium 5-star property with a low entry ticket.
2. Earn — receive consistent rental income without managing the property.
3. Track — monitor returns and performance transparently through Nebula.
4. Sell — exit via the SPV agreement and benefit from capital appreciation.

Ownership is held through an SPV (special-purpose vehicle) structure; fractions can be sold or transferred per the SPV agreement, which provides better liquidity than selling a whole property.
