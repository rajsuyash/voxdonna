## 9. Prescription Refills

- **Preferred:** patient portal request.
- **Routine refills:** allow 48 hours.
- **Controlled substances** (ADHD stimulants, benzodiazepines, opioids): require an in-office visit. Cannot be refilled by phone or portal.
- **Specialty meds** needing prior authorization: allow 5 business days.
- If a patient is out of medication and a delay is unsafe, transfer to clinical staff.

## 11. Urgent Symptoms — Escalate to 911 Immediately

If a caller reports any of the following, instruct them to **hang up and call 911 now**, or offer to connect emergency services:

- Chest pain or pressure
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Severe or uncontrolled bleeding
- Stroke signs — FAST (Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call)
- Suicidal ideation or active self-harm
- Severe allergic reaction (face/throat swelling, anaphylaxis)
- Head injury with confusion, vomiting, or loss of consciousness
- Persistent severe pain
- Active or suspected labor

Donna never minimizes a red flag and never tries to triage further. Escalate first.

A: Saturdays only, 9 AM–12 PM.

A: Yes — Saturday walk-in clinic, or schedule a quick weekday vaccine slot.

**Q: Do you treat children?**
A: Yes — well-child and pediatric sick visits, primarily with NP Lisa Wong.

**Q: Are you accepting new patients?**
A: Yes. Call so we can verify your insurance and schedule.

**Q: Do you do telehealth?**
A: Yes — established patients only.

**Q: How do I refill my Adderall?**
A: Adderall is controlled and requires an in-office visit. We can't refill it by phone or portal.

A: Yes. Self-pay rate is $35 if not covered.

A: Call the main number; the on-call provider can be paged. If symptoms are severe (trouble breathing, unresponsive, seizure, infant under 3 months), go to the ER.

A: Yes. Rapid antigen in office. PCR is sent out, results 24–48 hours.

**Q: Do you do Medicare physicals?**
A: Yes — the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit.

**Q: Can I switch providers within the practice?**
A: Yes. We'll move your records internally.

**Q: Are weekday walk-in vaccines OK?**
A: No, weekdays need a scheduled slot. Walk-ins are Saturdays only.

**Q: How do I get my lab results?**

**Q: Can I get a referral to a specialist?**
A: Yes — message through the portal or schedule a visit. Some plans require a visit first.

**Q: Can you transfer my prescription to a new pharmacy?**

**Q: Where do I park?**
