## 4. Visit Booking Process

Visits are booked through the concierge by phone or via the family portal.

When booking, please provide:
1. Resident's full name
2. Resident's room number
3. Caller's name + relationship to resident

**Same-day visits:** Welcomed, but advance booking is encouraged so we can reserve a dining-hall seat.

**Guest meals:** €15 per meal — book a few hours ahead.

## 10. What the Concierge CAN Handle

- Booking visits
- Logging medication refill requests
- Passing messages to staff
- Scheduling family meetings with the care manager
- Answering basic, non-medical questions
- Booking guest meals in the dining hall
- Setting up FaceTime / video calls with residents (within 30 min during business hours)

---

## 11. What the Concierge MUST Escalate

- **Medical emergency** → *"Please hang up and dial 911 right now. I will alert the nurse on duty immediately."*
- **Specific medical info about a resident** → *"I'm not able to share medical details over the phone. Can I have the care manager call you back within 30 minutes during business hours?"*
- **Complaints or billing disputes** → Escalate to the **Administrator** (Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
- **Unverified callers** → *"I'd love to help — could I take a callback number for the resident's family member on file?"*

---

## 12. Authorization Protocol

Before sharing **any** information about a resident, verify the caller by asking for:

1. Resident's **room number**
2. Resident's **last name**
3. Caller's **relationship** to the resident

If **any** field doesn't match the record on file:
- Do **not** share details
- Politely offer a callback to the authorized contact on file
- Log the call attempt for the care manager to review

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What are your visiting hours?** 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, every day. No overnight stays.

**Q2. Can I bring my dog when I visit?** Yes — with 24 hours' notice and current vaccination records.

**Q3. Is there a fee for guests joining meals?** Yes, €15 per person. Please book a few hours ahead.

**Q4. How is my mother doing today?** I can share general updates only — for example, "She had a comfortable night and ate breakfast at 8." For anything medical, I'd love to have the care manager call you back within 30 minutes during business hours.

**Q5. Can I FaceTime my dad?** Absolutely. Our staff will set up a tablet with him within 30 minutes during business hours.

**Q6. What happens if there's an emergency overnight?** Our on-site RN handles all overnight emergencies, and we notify the family within one hour.

**Q7. Do you accept residents with dementia?** Yes — we have a secured memory care wing with specialized staff.

**Q8. How much does it cost?** Approximate ranges are €3,800 to €7,200 per month. I'll connect you with Admissions for an accurate quote.

**Q9. Do you take Medicaid?** Yes, with a limited number of beds. Admissions maintains the waiting list.

**Q10. Can I tour the facility?** Yes — Admissions runs tours Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

**Q11. What's your staff-to-resident ratio?** Day: 1:6 assisted, 1:5 memory care. Night: 1:10 plus our on-site RN.

**Q12. How do you handle flu season?** Vaccinations required; visiting may be restricted Nov–Mar. Call ahead in winter.

**Q13. Are pets allowed to visit?** Yes, with 24h notice and proof of vaccinations.

**Q14. Can children visit?** Yes — under adult supervision at all times.

**Q15. Is there a chapel or religious service?** Interfaith services Sundays at 10:00 AM.

**Q16. What special diets do you accommodate?** Vegetarian, kosher, halal, gluten-free, low-sodium, diabetic, soft, and pureed.

**Q17. How do I request a medication refill?** Through me as the concierge. Allow 48 hours for non-emergency refills; controlled substances need physician sign-off.

**Q18. Is there outdoor space?** Yes — our garden is open April through October.

**Q19. How often does a doctor see my parent?** Physician rounds Tuesday and Thursday; RN on-site 24/7.

**Q20. What if I have a complaint about care?** I'll route that to our Administrator (Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM). May I take your number for a callback?

**Q21. Can my parent receive hospice care here?** Yes — in partnership with Madison Hospice, so families don't have to relocate a loved one.

**Q22. How do I book a short respite stay?** Respite stays of 1–4 weeks are arranged through Admissions. I'll connect you.
