## Restaurant Overview

Ember & Oak is a fine-dining restaurant in the Marais, Paris, blending modern French technique with American wood-fire cooking. The 64-seat room features oak beams and an open hearth. Chef Camille Roux leads the kitchen.

- Address: 47 Rue de Saint Antoine, Paris 75004
- Phone: +33 1 42 33 56 78
- Cuisine: Modern French-American, wood-fire focused
- Average spend: 95 to 130 euros per person, food only

## Hours and Reservations

## Dietary Accommodations

- Gluten-free: risotto, branzino, ribeye, lamb, duck, and cauliflower are naturally GF.
- Vegan: cauliflower steak, eggplant curry, tomato tartare, beet carpaccio without cheese, coconut panna cotta. Full vegan tasting menu with 24-hour notice.
- Nut allergies: kitchen handles nuts. Safe versions available — flag at booking.
- Shellfish allergies: bouillabaisse, scallop, and octopus contain shellfish. Separate boards and pans for allergic guests.
- The chef adapts any dish for serious allergies with 24 hours notice.

## Dress Code

Smart casual. Collared shirts, blouses, dresses, tailored denim, dress shoes or clean sneakers welcome. No athleisure, gym wear, or shorts. Jackets not required.

## Kids Policy

Children welcome. Kids menu 18 euros: grilled chicken with frites, buttered tagliatelle, or steak haché, plus juice and a scoop of ice cream. High chairs available. Strollers stay at the host stand.

## Private Events and Groups

- Parties of 8+: 25 euro per-person deposit at booking, applied to the final bill.
- Private dining room seats up to 24 with dedicated server and sommelier.
- Full buyouts Sunday and Tuesday from 9,500 euros food and beverage minimum.
- Pre-set group menus required for parties of 12 or more.

## Parking and Accessibility

- Street parking on Rue de Saint Antoine and Rue de Birague (paid 9am to 8pm).
- Indigo Parc Saint Paul garage, two blocks away on Rue de Rivoli.
- Ground-floor dining room and restroom are wheelchair accessible. Mezzanine by stairs only.
- Staff trained to assist hearing-impaired guests. Large-print and Braille menus on request.
- Closest Metro: Saint-Paul, Line 1, three minutes on foot.
