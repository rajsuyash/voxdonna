## li-006 — Why chatbots fail at voice
Most companies tried chatbots first. That was the wrong order.

Text chat forces a customer to type what is wrong. Voice lets them say it. Most inbound support volume still arrives by phone.

We have seen brands process 2,500 calls a month with 3 people. Voice AI handles 98% without touching a human. The other 2% go to the person with the patience and context to actually help.

That is not automation. That is a better phone channel.

## li-010 — Scope before architecture
The fastest way to guarantee a failed voice AI project is to call it a voice AI project.

The teams getting this right call it a customer operations project. They pull the call log. They find the 6 question types that account for most of the volume. They build the agent around those 6 things and nothing else.

Scope before architecture. Every time.
