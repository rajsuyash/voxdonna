02 — What your company owns

Built in your accounts.
Owned by your company.

Most voice AI is bought as a service. We deliver a capability your business controls, then manage it for you.

A · Infrastructure

Your accounts, your keys

Runs in your company's cloud, with hosting, telephony and model-provider accounts in your name. You hold admin access and set permissions, retention and exports.

B · Intellectual property

Your playbook, in code

Scripts, qualification rules, escalation logic and integrations are assigned to your company, with source code, configuration and documentation delivered.

C · Data

Your call records

Every call record is available for your own quality reviews and analytics. You approve which speech, model and telephony providers may process calls.

D · Continuity

Ready for a change of owner

A handover package, access register and tested exports mean a new operator, or a new owner, starts from documented rules rather than rediscovery.
