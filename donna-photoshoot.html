The problem

Your catalogue moves faster than your camera.

New designs land every week. A shoot needs a model, a studio, a stylist, a photographer, a location permit, and a calendar where all six agree.

📅

The shoot is a bottleneck

Pieces sit unphotographed for weeks, so they sit unsold. Marketing runs on whatever was shot last quarter.

💰

Cost per look is brutal

Model day rate, crew, location, post. Then a designer needs one more angle and the whole line item repeats.

📱

Product-on-white doesn't sell

Nobody buys a necklace off a cut-out. They buy it worn, on a face, in a room they'd want to be in.

How it works

Upload. Pick. Publish.

Three choices and a few minutes. The piece in the output is the piece you uploaded, not something that resembles it.

Step 01

Upload the piece

One product photo, however you already shoot it. The system reads what it is and where it is worn — neck, ear, wrist, hand — so the framing is right without you saying so.

Step 02

Cast and set

A fixed cast of Indian models, spread across regions and skin tones, with a persistent face — the same model across every campaign. Fifty locations from Amber Fort to Lake Como.

Step 03

Three framings back

Hero, profile and detail, delivered as they finish. Reshoot any single framing for one credit — the seed changes, so you get a genuinely different take, not the same image again.

The sets

Fifty locations. No permits.

How it bills

One credit, one image.

Not a bundle, not a plan you grow out of. A shoot is three credits, a reshoot is one, a retouch is one. When two of three framings come back and the third fails, you are refunded exactly one credit — no rounding, no argument, no support ticket.

1
Prepaid, with a GST invoice

Buy credits, get the invoice against your GSTIN. Your finance team files it like any other vendor bill.

2
The ledger is append-only

Every reserve, settle and refund is a row. The balance is derived from the ledger, and a daily reconcile flags a disagreement the day it happens, not at audit.

3
Failures refund themselves

Credits are charged before generation and released automatically when an image does not arrive. Nothing is billed for work you never received.
