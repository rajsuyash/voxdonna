How it works

One number.
Every thread answered.

It runs on the WhatsApp Business number your customers already save, not a new one you have to teach them.

01

It reads what actually arrived

Text, voice note, or a photo of a piece the customer wants something like. Voice notes matter here — a large share of Indian WhatsApp enquiries are spoken, not typed.

02

It answers from your approved information

Collections, branch stock, the day's rate, scheme terms, exchange and hallmarking policy. Your words, not a general-purpose model's guess.

03

It sends the pictures

Jewellery is bought with the eyes. The agent sends the collection images for what was asked about, from the catalogue your marketing team maintains.

04

It qualifies without interrogating

Occasion, rough budget, preferred branch, the date it is needed for. Four things your advisor needs, asked the way a good salesperson would.

05

It books, and confirms in the thread

The consultation goes in the advisor's diary and the confirmation stays in the same conversation the customer started.

06

It follows up when the thread goes quiet

The enquiry that never came in, the visit that did not convert, the instalment that is late. Chased on a schedule, until there is an answer or an opt-out.

Template messages go through approved WhatsApp Business templates, the 24-hour window is respected, and STOP is honoured on every thread. Opt-outs are permanent and recorded.

Questions jewellers ask

What jewellers ask
about the WhatsApp number.

Can an AI agent reply to jewellery enquiries on WhatsApp?

Yes, on the business number your customers already message. It answers catalogue, rate, branch availability, exchange, repair and scheme questions from your approved information, sends collection images, qualifies the enquiry on occasion and budget, and books a consultation. Anything outside what you have approved goes to a person rather than being guessed at.

Will it work with the WhatsApp number we already use?

That is the intention — a new number means teaching every existing customer a new number, which defeats the point. It runs on your WhatsApp Business account through approved templates, respects the 24-hour session window, and honours STOP permanently on any thread.

Can it handle voice notes and Hinglish?

Both, and it needs to. A large share of Indian WhatsApp enquiries arrive as voice notes rather than typed text, and customers write half in Hindi and half in English in the same line. The agent handles ten languages including Hinglish, in text and voice, and replies in whatever the customer used.

Can it chase gold savings scheme instalments?

Yes, and it is one of the clearest wins because the work is pure repetition on a schedule. The agent messages before the due date and follows up after a missed one, answers what is due and what has been paid from your scheme ledger, and records the response so your team is chasing only the people who need chasing.

What happens with bridal enquiries that run over weeks?

They stay in one thread. A bridal order typically runs three to five showroom visits with design revisions and a fitting, and the common failure is a week where nobody replied. The agent keeps the conversation alive between visits, tracks the promised design and the next appointment, and hands to your advisor when a decision is due. More on that in turning enquiries into showroom visits.

Does it send images, or only text?

Images, which matters when the product is jewellery. It sends the collection photos for whatever was asked about, from the catalogue your marketing team already maintains, so the customer is choosing from your pieces rather than describing someone else's.

How do we stop it from answering something it should not?

By scoping what it knows. It answers from your catalogue, rate sheet, stock extract and policy text, and refuses anything outside them. Valuations, disputes and bespoke commissions are routed to a person with the thread attached.
