How it works

What happens
between the ring and the CRM.

One call, the same path every time, and a written record at the end of it.

01

It answers on the first ring

No queue, no hold music, no voicemail. The caller starts talking to something that responds, in the language they opened with.

02

It works out what they actually want

Rate, availability, timings, scheme, repair, or a bridal consultation. The intent decides the path, not a phone-tree menu.

03

It answers from your approved data

The day's rate, your store timings, your scheme terms, your stock extract. If the answer is not in there, it says so rather than inventing one.

04

It books the visit or the callback

Real availability against your advisors' diaries, the slot confirmed on the call and again on WhatsApp so the customer has it in writing.

05

It writes the record

Who called, what they asked, the occasion and budget if given, what was promised. Your advisor opens a note, not a recording.

06

It hands over when it should

A complaint, a high-value bespoke enquiry, an unhappy customer. Escalated to a person with the context already captured.

Nothing is installed in your billing or ERP system to start. A scheduled export of rates, stock and scheme data is enough for a first line; a live connection makes the answers fresher and can come later.

Questions jewellers ask

What jewellers ask
about the phone line.

Can an AI voice agent answer gold rate enquiries for my showroom?

Yes, and it is usually the first thing a jeweller automates because it is the highest-volume, lowest-judgement call on the line. The agent quotes the rate your team has approved for that day from the rate sheet or board you already publish, and sends it on WhatsApp so the customer has it in writing. It does not calculate or negotiate a price; anything beyond the published rate goes to an advisor.

What happens to calls that come in after the showroom closes?

They get answered. A wedding family deciding on a Sunday morning will not wait until Monday, and an unanswered ring at 9pm is a customer calling the next jeweller. The agent takes the enquiry out of hours, answers what it can from your approved information, and books a slot or a callback for when your team is next on the floor.

Will it handle callers who do not speak English?

One agent covers Hindi, English, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati and Bengali, and switches mid-call when the caller does. That matters in Indian jewellery retail because the language a customer opens in is rarely the language they finish in.

Can it actually book a showroom appointment, or just take a message?

It books. The agent checks real availability against your advisors' diaries, confirms the slot on the call, and sends the confirmation on WhatsApp. Your advisor gets the occasion, the budget if the customer gave one, and the branch — not a callback request. Appointment flow in detail is on the showroom appointment booking page.

Do we need to connect it to our billing or ERP software first?

No. A scheduled export of your rate sheet, stock and scheme data is enough to answer the common calls from day one, and nothing is installed inside your existing system to run a pilot. A live connection makes answers fresher and can be added once you have seen it work.

What stops it from giving a customer the wrong price or the wrong promise?

It answers only from the data you approve, and the refusal path is designed before the answer path. A question it cannot answer from your information is handed to a person with the context captured, rather than guessed at. An agent that invents a making charge costs more than the call it saved.

How does it handle Dhanteras and wedding-season peaks?

That is when it earns its keep. Festival and wedding demand concentrates months of calls into a few days, and that is exactly when your floor is least able to pick up the phone. The agent takes the overflow so the queue standing in your showroom gets your advisors' attention.
