How it works

It answers from your data, or it says so.

An agent that invents a ship date costs more than the call it saved. The design point is what happens when it does not know.

Step 01

Your part and order data

A nightly export is enough to begin: open orders, stages, part master, stock. A live ERP connection makes it fresher and can come later.

Step 02

The call, handled end to end

It greets, identifies the caller, finds the order or part, answers, and confirms on WhatsApp where that helps. It interrupts politely and waits like a person does.

Step 03

A record, not a recording

Every call leaves structured fields: intent, order or part, what was promised, and whether a human is needed. Your team reads outcomes and acts, instead of listening back.
