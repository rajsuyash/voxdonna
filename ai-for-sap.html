How it connects

The integration question, answered honestly.

Most AI for SAP stalls in the same meeting: basis says no live connection this quarter. So the agents are built to earn their keep without one, and to get better when you grant one.

1
Files, to start

Scheduled exports into a folder. Nothing is installed in SAP, nothing changes, no transport is needed. Most pilots never go further than this.

2
A service, when you are ready

An OData or RFC endpoint, or whatever your middleware already exposes, gives the agent live status instead of this morning's picture.

3
Write access, only where you want it

Creating a sales order is the one write most teams want. It stays behind validation, and anything that fails is held for a person.

4
Read-only by default

Every query path is read-only, row-capped and killed on timeout. A badly phrased question cannot touch your data or your bill.

Questions we get

What SAP teams ask first.

Q

Can an AI voice agent really work with SAP?
Yes. It answers the call and reads or writes through the access your landscape allows: a service, middleware, or a scheduled export. The caller gets an answer either way.

Q

Do we need an integration project first?
No. Reporting runs on exports, order entry runs on the mailbox. A live connection is an upgrade, not a prerequisite.

Q

ECC or S/4HANA?
Both, and Business One. The integration point is a service, a mailbox or a file, never a screen, so the release does not matter.

Q

Which processes are covered?
Order entry from customer POs, order status and ETA, spare parts, warranty intake, and questions on FI, SD and MM reporting data.

Q

How do you stop it inventing a number?
The copilot never writes its own SQL; it fills a spec of metrics that exist in your catalog, so an unmapped question is refused. The order agent holds anything that fails validation.

Q

How fast is a pilot?
Weeks, not quarters. Send one folder of exports or five real purchase orders, and the first working version comes back on your own data.

Bring one process. We'll bring it back working.

Pick the one that costs you the most people: the order mailbox, the status calls, or the report nobody has time to build. A pilot runs on your own data, not a slide.
